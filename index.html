About Second Volt

Second Volt is a student-run store that tests, grades and resells used phones and laptops. Every device is checked by hand, wiped, and described honestly, so you know the condition before you buy.

We keep good electronics out of landfill and offer them at fair prices, with a 12 month warranty on every order.
